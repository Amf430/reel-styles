# برومبتات الحلقة ١ (Google Flow، Nano Banana Pro، 9:16، ×2)

البرومبتات هنا مثل ما انكتبت بالضبط. اللقطة النهائية لكل مشهد تحت اسمها، والشبكات (٤ و٨) انقصت بـ `split-grid.py` وانرفقت كمرجع لبرومبتات «Recreate».

## 1. شيت البطل (Ingredient «Hero»)
```
Young Saudi man, 27, the main character. Stylized black ink comic character: perfectly round smooth white head like a mannequin, only two small black dot eyes, no hair, no nose, no realistic face. Dark charcoal hoodie, black slim pants, white sneakers. Quiet, observant, nervous but brave.
```

## 2. شيت العم (Ingredient «Uncle»)
```
Saudi man, 58, head of the family, the hero's uncle. Stylized black ink comic character: smooth white egg-shaped head like a mannequin, only two small black dot eyes and stern eyebrow lines, short grey stubble on the chin, no nose, no realistic face. Wears a white thobe, a red and white checkered shemagh with a black agal ring, black prayer beads. The red shemagh is the only red color in any image. Calm, heavy, controlling, hides secrets.
```

## 3. تجربة: لقطة مجلس وحدة
```
Vertical 9:16 comic panel. Use the characters Hero and Uncle exactly as in their references (round white mannequin heads, dot eyes, no nose, same clothes). Copy the art style and colors of the window/street image exactly: black ink, dense crosshatching, paper grain, charcoal grey with warm amber as the only accent color; Uncle's red shemagh is the only red. Scene: a traditional Saudi majlis in the early morning, Hero and Uncle sit on floor cushions facing each other with a brass coffee tray between them. Uncle leans forward toward Hero, a smartphone lies face up on the tray, its screen glowing amber. Hero leans back slightly, nervous. Tense silence. No text, no speech bubbles, no border.
```

Characters Hero and Uncle exactly as in their references (round white mannequin heads, two dot eyes, no nose, same clothes). No text anywhere. Panels: 1) dark bedroom at 2 AM, a smartphone vibrating on the bedside table, screen glowing amber. 2) Hero lying in bed, phone light on his face, shocked. 3) close-up of the phone screen showing a voice message waveform. 4) Hero at the window seen from behind, looking down at the street. 5) a white SUV parked across the street at night, headlights off, a dark silhouette inside. 6) early morning, Uncle alone in a traditional Saudi majlis pouring coffee from a dallah, two cups on the tray. 7) Uncle holding a small cup out toward the viewer, eyes fixed on the viewer. 8) Uncle placing his smartphone face up on the tray. 9) extreme close-up of Uncle's dot eyes narrowing, half of his face in shadow.
